PasteGuard

What PasteGuard sees

Detection runs on your device. The only things that leave it are a package name sent to npm or PyPI, the placeholder text you send to the AI site, and, if you remove the extension and tap an answer, one number.

DataWhere it livesWho can see itHow long
Pasted textguard.ts Only in the tab while you paste. Never saved. You Until the paste is checked
Secret valuesvault.ts Tab memory and storage.session, which Chrome keeps in memory only You Until the tab closes
Placeholdersdetect.js The AI site, as PG_SECRET_1 You and the AI site As long as the AI site keeps your chats
Allow liststorage.ts SHA-256 hashes in storage.local, never the values You Until you remove them
Weekly countsstorage.ts Counts by type in storage.local, no content You 14 days
Package namesregistry.ts Sent to npm or PyPI, with no cookies or referrer. The answer is cached on your device. The registry, in its logs Registry: their policy. Your device: 24 hours
Uninstall reasonbye.js Our storage: the option you tapped and the extension version, no email or IP Us 90 days

The trust model, drawn

Coral packets are real secrets and never cross the dotted device boundary. Yellow packets are placeholders, and they are the only thing the AI site receives.

The extension is open source. Each row above links to the file that handles that data.
