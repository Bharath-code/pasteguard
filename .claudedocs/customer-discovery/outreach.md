# Outreach messages

Rules:
- Ask for learning, not a sale.
- Name the specific signal you saw.
- Keep it under 80 words and make one ask.
- Send each message yourself, personalized. No bulk sends.
- Never claim users, customers or results we don't have.

## 1. Warm ask to your network
> Hey {name}, quick one. I'm researching how small SaaS teams handle security reviews now that customers ask about AI use. Do you know anyone (CTO, eng lead, security lead) going through SOC 2 or answering security questionnaires right now? A 20-minute chat would help me a lot, and I'll share what I learn across teams.

## 2. vCISO / SOC 2 consultant
> Hi {name}, I saw you help startups through SOC 2. I'm compiling the AI questions that now show up in security questionnaires, especially about staff use of ChatGPT and similar tools, plus model answers. Would you trade 20 minutes of what you're seeing for an early copy? Happy to credit you or keep it anonymous.

## 3. Cold LinkedIn (connection note, ≤ 300 chars)
> Hi {name}, saw {company} {signal: "is hiring a GRC analyst" / "shared its SOC 2 report" / "has a Vanta trust center"}. I'm researching how teams answer the new AI questions in security questionnaires. Open to a 20-min chat? Not selling. I'll share the compiled question list.

## 4. Follow-up (day 3, once)
> Following up once. I'm collecting real examples of AI questions in security questionnaires (anonymized). If a 20-min chat doesn't fit, even a forwarded redacted AI section would help, and I'll send you the full compilation.

## 5. Community post (SaaSBoomi / r/SaaS / Indie Hackers)
> **What AI questions are you getting in security questionnaires?**
> I'm compiling the AI sections that now show up in SIG / CAIQ / custom questionnaires, especially questions about how your own team uses ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot. If you've answered one recently, what did they ask and what did you answer? I'll post the anonymized compilation with model answers back here.

## 6. Booking confirmation
> Thanks! Here's a link for 20 minutes: {calendar}. No prep needed. If you have a recent questionnaire with AI questions, having it open would help (you don't need to share it).

## 7. Thank-you (same day)
> Thanks for your time today, it was really useful. {one specific thing they said}. I'll send the AI-questionnaire compilation when it's ready. And if anyone else comes to mind who's in the middle of SOC 2, I'd love an intro.
